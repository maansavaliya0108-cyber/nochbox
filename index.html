Our coffee

The craft in the cup.

Specialty-grade beans, pulled as espresso, brewed by hand or steeped cold.

  1. 01

    Espresso

    Espresso, americano, flat white, cappuccino, latte, mocha and espresso tonic — several hot or iced.

  2. 02

    Brewed by hand

    Pour over, AeroPress or moka pot. The printed menu suggests asking the barista for bean options.

  3. 03

    Cold brew

    Classic, with tonic, with ginger ale, or with cold foam.
